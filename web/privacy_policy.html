3. How User Data Is Handled, Processed & Used

All data processing is restricted strictly to academic and service-delivery functions:

  • Automated Subject Vault Matching: The extension matches extracted course codes with our verified repository of 402 university course study guides and past papers.
  • Academic Deadline Sync: Assignment and quiz submission deadlines are converted into calendar events and synced to Google Calendar only when explicitly confirmed by the student.
  • Private Cloud Backup: Study notes and quiz progress are backed up directly into the user's own Google Drive isolated AppData folder, ensuring that the user maintains complete ownership of their data.
  • AI Academic Explanations: Questions submitted to AI are processed ephemerally to generate explanations; they are not used for advertising or model training.
  • Peer Community Collaboration: Verified student IDs ensure that discussion rooms remain free of commercial spammers and bots.

4. Data Storage, Retention & Security Protections

We implement robust technical and organizational safeguards to ensure student data is fully protected:

  • Local Storage: UI preferences, cached course lists, and offline bookmarks reside strictly within the browser's local sandbox (chrome.storage.local) and are never shared with unauthorized parties.
  • Google Drive AppData: Backups are saved in Google's secure cloud within the isolated appDataFolder scope. This folder is hidden from other applications and accessible only by HM Nexora.
  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of network communications between the extension, Google APIs, Groq AI, and our backend use industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption with Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud databases (Supabase) store user profile and community data with enterprise AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Data Retention Timelines:
    • Local Browser Data: Retained until the user clears extension cache, resets settings, or uninstalls the extension.
    • Google Drive AppData: Retained in the user's Google Drive until manually deleted by the user or when Google account access is revoked.
    • AI Query Data: Processed ephemerally in RAM by Groq AI and discarded immediately after generating the response (zero retention).
    • Community Chat Messages: Retained for educational reference; can be deleted upon request at any time.
