🎓 HM Nexora — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026 | App Owner: Haseeb Saleem (haseebsaleem312@gmail.com)
Summary: HM Nexora is an educational companion platform for Virtual University students. We respect your privacy, protect your data with end-to-end HTTPS encryption, and will never sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you use HM Nexora, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, Email Address, and Virtual University Student ID provided during registration or OAuth login (Google / Facebook).
  • User Contributed Content: Study materials, handouts, solved past papers, or notes uploaded to the HM Nexora Study Vault.
  • Usage & Telemetry Data: App interaction logs, crash reports, and study planner entries to optimize user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly used for educational services, including:

  • Authenticating your account and maintaining your personal Study Vault.
  • Organizing course-specific study resources (e.g. CS407, MGT502, ENG101).
  • Providing AI-assisted mentoring and quiz preparation features.

3. Third-Party Services

HM Nexora integrates with trusted third-party providers:

  • Supabase: Secure database storage and authentication.
  • Google Identity Services: Optional Google Sign-In authentication.
  • Meta / Facebook Login: Optional Facebook Sign-In authentication.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You can view or update your profile information in Settings or request account deletion anytime by emailing haseebsaleem312@gmail.com.
